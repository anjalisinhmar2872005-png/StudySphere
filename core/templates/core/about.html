About StudySphere

StudySphere is a student-focused academic productivity platform designed to help students organize their studies, manage tasks, track progress and work towards their academic goals.

Why StudySphere?

Students often manage subjects, assignments, study schedules and academic goals using different tools. StudySphere brings these activities together into one organized platform.

📚

Organize

Manage subjects, topics, study plans and academic tasks from one place.

📊

Track Progress

Monitor topic completion, study activity, tasks and academic progress.

🎯

Achieve Goals

Set academic goals and stay focused on your study targets.

How StudySphere Helps

StudySphere combines planning, tracking and analytics to give students a clearer view of their academic journey. Features such as Smart Priority and Progress Analytics help students understand what needs attention.

About the Developer

StudySphere is developed as an academic project using Python and Django, with database management and modern web technologies.
